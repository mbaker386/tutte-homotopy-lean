# Mathematical source and development

The mathematical source is *A modern perspective on Tutte's homotopy theorem*
by Matthew Baker, Tong Jin, and Oliver Lorscheid, with an appendix by Juš Kocutar,
re-expressing Tutte's arguments in the lattice-of-flats language.

The formalization was developed in a local Lean/Mathlib project with Codex
assistance, coordinated by Matthew Baker. ChatGPT assisted with mathematical
review, statement comparison, and proof-to-manuscript reconciliation. These tools
are acknowledged as tools, not listed as human authors or independent human
certifiers. The mathematical authors' names above describe the source paper;
the software citation author list should be confirmed separately by its maintainers.

Lean and Mathlib supply the proof-checking environment and substantial mathematical
infrastructure. This repository is a separate companion project, not a contribution
already incorporated into Mathlib and not an endorsement by its maintainers.

The publication-preparation package preserves the complete mathematical source
from the reviewed final archive. It omits private working history and drafts;
its `SOURCE_SNAPSHOT.json` records the originating archive hash and file hashes.
